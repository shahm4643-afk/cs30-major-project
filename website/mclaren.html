Norris — The People's Champion

Lando Norris has become one of the most loved drivers on the grid — quick, charismatic, and relentlessly entertaining both on and off the track. Since joining McLaren, he has steadily grown into one of the team’s key figures, known for extracting strong performances even in difficult machinery. After years of near-misses and heartbreak, 2024 marked his true arrival as a championship contender. His long-awaited first victory at the Miami Grand Prix was celebrated across the entire paddock and signaled a major turning point in his career. With McLare’s MCL38 becoming the class of the field by mid-season, Norris consistently challenged at the front and pushed Max Verstappen deep into the title fight, taking the championship battle down to the final rounds of the season. His combination of speed, racecraft, and resilience has firmly established him as one of Formula 1's leading drivers.

Piastri — The Quiet Assassin

Oscar Piastri arrived at McLaren in 2023 with relatively little fanfare but quickly established himself as one of the most impressive young talents on the grid. Calm under pressure, precise in execution, and consistently fast over a race distance, Piastri adapted to Formula 1 with remarkable speed and maturity. After a strong debut season, he took a major step forward in 2024, consistently challenging at the front and securing his maiden victory at the Hungarian Grand Prix with a composed and intelligent drive that showcased his racecraft and control. Since then, Piastri has continued to develop into a key figure within McLaren’s resurgence, combining natural speed with strong tyre management and race awareness. At just 23, he is widely regarded as one of the most complete and promising young drivers in Formula 1.

Team Legacy

The Story of Papaya Excellence

1963: Founded

Bruce McLaren founded the team at just 25 years old. A Kiwi engineer-driver with a vision, he built one of motorsport's most enduring institutions from the ground up.

1974-76: Fittipaldi & Hunt

Emerson Fittipaldi delivered McLaren's first Drivers' title in 1974. Two years later, James Hunt's charismatic championship battle with Lauda in 1976 became one of the most iconic seasons in F1 history.

1984-91: The Senna & Prost Era

The golden age. Ayrton Senna and Alain Prost, together and apart, drove McLaren to six Constructors' titles and seven Drivers' championships. The MP4/4 won 15 of 16 races in 1988 — a record that stood for 35 years.

1998-99: Häkkinen's Titles

Mika Häkkinen delivered back-to-back World Championships, proving McLaren could still win at the highest level in the modern era.

2021-23: The Rebuild

Under Zak Brown and Andreas Seidl, McLaren rebuilt from the midfield back to championship contention. Norris and Piastri became one of the most exciting driver pairings on the grid.

2024: Constructors' Champions

McLaren ended a 26-year wait to lift the Constructors' Championship, with Norris and Piastri combining brilliantly to overhaul Red Bull in the second half of the season.
