Leclerc's Ferrari Dream

Charles Leclerc has been the heart of Ferrari’s resurgence since joining the team in 2019. A childhood Ferrari fan turned factory driver, he quickly established himself as one of the most naturally gifted qualifiers in modern Formula 1, delivering electric one-lap performances that have defined many of Ferrari’s strongest moments in recent years. With 20 pole positions and eight Grand Prix victories, Leclerc has consistently demonstrated the raw speed needed to compete at the very highest level. His ability to extract performance from the car in qualifying has made him one of the most feared drivers over a single lap on the grid. His 2022 season marked a major breakthrough, as he led the championship for much of the year and pushed Max Verstappen harder than any other driver in the title fight. Despite setbacks, Leclerc’s performances confirmed his status as a genuine championship contender and a central figure in Ferrari’s long-term ambitions.

Lewis Hamilton's New Chapter

The most decorated driver in Formula 1 history joins Ferrari for the 2025 season, fulfilling a long-held childhood dream. Lewis Hamilton — seven-time World Champion, with 105 race wins and 104 pole positions — brings an unmatched level of experience, success, and global influence to the Scuderia. Hamilton’s arrival marks one of the most significant driver moves in modern Formula 1 history, pairing him with the sport’s most iconic team. His reputation for racecraft, consistency, and strategic intelligence makes him a transformative addition to Ferrari’s ambitions. The partnership between Hamilton and Ferrari carries enormous weight, blending legacy, expectation, and passion from an entire fanbase. As he enters the next chapter of his career, Hamilton aims to chase further success while helping return Ferrari to championship-winning form.

Team Legacy

The Oldest, The Greatest, The Prancing Horse

1950: The Beginning

Ferrari entered Formula 1 at the very first World Championship season. Founded by Enzo Ferrari, the Scuderia became the soul of the sport before the sport even knew what it was.

1952-53: Ascari's Dominance

Alberto Ascari delivered back-to-back World Championships for Ferrari, establishing the team as the benchmark of early Formula 1 excellence.

1975-79: Lauda & Scheckter

Niki Lauda brought Ferrari back to the top with two championships, before Jody Scheckter sealed the 1979 title — Ferrari's last Constructors' crown for two decades.

2000-04: The Schumacher Era

Michael Schumacher and Ferrari produced the most dominant stretch in F1 history at the time — five consecutive Drivers' titles and six Constructors' Championships. An era that redefined what dominance looked like.

2022: The Return

The SF-75 announced Ferrari's serious return as championship contenders. Leclerc led the standings for months and the team won six races, reminding the world the Prancing Horse never truly leaves.

2025: Hamilton Arrives

Lewis Hamilton joins Charles Leclerc at Ferrari — a partnership of generational talent that carries the dreams of millions of tifosi around the world.
