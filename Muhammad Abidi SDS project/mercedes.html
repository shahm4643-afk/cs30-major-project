Russell — Mr. Saturday

George Russell arrived at Mercedes in 2022 as one of the most technically astute drivers on the grid. Nicknamed "Mr. Saturday" for his exceptional qualifying pace, Russell quickly established himself as a key figure within the team. From his first season, he consistently matched and often outperformed expectations, delivering strong results even under difficult circumstances. One of the standout moments of his early Mercedes career came with victory at the 2022 Brazilian Grand Prix in just his second season with the team, showcasing his ability to compete and win at the highest level. Since then, Russell has continued to grow in confidence, becoming one of the most consistent performers on the grid. Known for his precision, adaptability, and strong race management, he has also taken on an increasingly leadership-focused role within the team. His feedback and technical understanding have made him an important part of Mercedes’ development direction.

Antonelli — The Next Generation

Son of sportscar racer Marco, the Bologna native was spotted by Mercedes during a karting career where he collected a huge number of wins and trophies. He made his car racing debut at just 15 and quickly added more titles, winning both the Italian and ADAC F4 championships in 2022, followed by Formula Regional Middle East and European titles a year later. While most expected him to move into F3, Mercedes chose to fast-track him into F2, skipping a step on the traditional ladder to better develop their young talent. The move brought pressure, especially with Lewis Hamilton announcing his switch to Ferrari in 2025, which opened a seat alongside George Russell. Antonelli focused on steady progress in F2, scoring consistent points before breaking through mid-season with standout performances.

Team Legacy

The Silver Arrows — A Century of Speed

1954: The Silver Arrows Return

Mercedes returned to Grand Prix racing in 1954 with the legendary W196, winning the Drivers' Championship with Juan Manuel Fangio in both 1954 and 1955 before withdrawing from motorsport.

2010: The Modern Era Begins

Mercedes re-entered F1 as a constructor, signing Michael Schumacher out of retirement. The foundations of what would become the most dominant team in modern F1 history were quietly being laid.

2014: The Hybrid Revolution

The new turbo-hybrid era handed Mercedes a technical advantage they exploited ruthlessly. Hamilton and Rosberg delivered the first of seven consecutive Constructors' titles — a run of dominance without equal.

2014-2020: Seven in a Row

Seven consecutive Constructors' Championships. Lewis Hamilton won six Drivers' titles in this period. No team in the history of the sport has sustained such dominance for so long.

2021: The Fight

Hamilton and Verstappen produced the greatest title battle in the modern era — going to the final lap of the final race. A season that defined a generation of F1 fans.

2025: A New Dawn

With Hamilton departed to Ferrari, George Russell leads a new era alongside teenage prodigy Kimi Antonelli. Mercedes rebuild with hunger and technical excellence, as always.
